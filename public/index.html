Stop writing, and the void takes it.

How it works

  1. 1
    WriteTyping starts the timer. Keep going.
  2. 2
    Pause 5 secondsYour text begins to fade.
  3. 3
    It's goneFaded text is deleted for good. There is no undo.

Good to know

  • Pass your word goal to unlock a plain .txt download. The timer pauses while you save.
  • Private by design. Your writing never leaves your device. No accounts, no ads.
  • Install it to your dock or home screen and it works fully offline.
