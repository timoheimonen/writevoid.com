About writevoid

Stop writing, and the void takes it.

writevoid is a minimalist, distraction-free writing app where your words are temporary by design. Keep typing and your draft grows. Stop, and it fades away for good.

The goal is simple: keep your flow by removing the urge to polish while you draft.

How it works

  1. 1
    WriteType in a clean editor right in your browser. No account needed.
  2. 2

FAQ

What is writevoid?

writevoid is a browser-based minimalist writing app where text starts to fade and then disappears if you stop typing.

What is the difference between focus and hardcore mode?

Both modes start fading your text after 5 seconds without typing. In focus mode the text fades out over 10 seconds, in hardcore mode over 5 seconds. Start typing again before it is gone and the text comes back.

Can I recover text that has faded away?

No. Once text has fully faded it is deleted permanently. To keep a draft, reach your word goal and download it as a .txt file.

Is writevoid free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login or profile system.

Can I use writevoid offline or on mobile?

Yes. It works in modern desktop and mobile browsers, and you can install it as a progressive web app that works offline after the first visit.
